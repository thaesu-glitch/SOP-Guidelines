## 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes the policy, principles, and detailed procedures governing the disposal of the Company's fixed assets. The objectives are to:

1. Ensure all disposals are properly **authorized, documented, and accounted for**.
2. **Safeguard Company assets** against loss, theft, misuse, or unauthorized disposal.
3. Maximize **economic value recovered** from disposed assets.
4. Ensure compliance with applicable **accounting standards** (e.g., IFRS / local GAAP), **tax regulations**, and **environmental / data-protection laws**.
5. Maintain accuracy and integrity of the **Fixed Asset Register (FAR)** and the General Ledger (GL).

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All **tangible fixed assets** capitalized in the FAR, including but not limited to: land, buildings, leasehold improvements, machinery, plant and equipment, furniture and fixtures, office equipment, IT hardware, motor vehicles, and tools.
- All **intangible assets** carried on the books (e.g., software licenses, capitalized development costs) where disposal or derecognition is contemplated.
- **All entities, departments, and locations** of the Company and its subsidiaries.
- All **employees, contractors, and third parties** involved in the requesting, approving, executing, or recording of asset disposals.

Inventory items, consumables, and stock-in-trade are **excluded** from this policy and governed by the Inventory Management SOP.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Fixed Asset** | A tangible or intangible asset with a useful life exceeding 12 months, capitalized in accordance with the Company's Capitalization Policy. |
| **Disposal** | The act of removing a fixed asset from active use and from the Fixed Asset Register, whether by sale, trade-in, donation, scrapping, write-off, transfer, loss, or destruction. |
| **Net Book Value (NBV)** | Original cost less accumulated depreciation, impairment, and any prior partial disposals. |
| **Fixed Asset Register (FAR)** | The subsidiary ledger maintained by Finance that records each capitalized asset with its identification, location, custodian, cost, depreciation, and current status. |
| **Custodian** | The department head or designated employee accountable for the use, condition, and physical safeguarding of a fixed asset. |
| **Disposal Committee** | A cross-functional committee constituted to review and recommend disposals above defined thresholds (see Section 6). |
| **DRF** | Disposal Request Form — the standard form used to initiate a disposal request. |

## 4. Policy Statements

1. **No fixed asset shall be disposed of without prior written approval** in accordance with the Authority Matrix in Section 6.
2. Disposals must be supported by a **valid business justification** (e.g., obsolescence, end of useful life, damage beyond economic repair, replacement, sale, change in business needs).
3. All disposals must be **recorded promptly** in the FAR and GL in the period in which the disposal occurs.
4. Where assets are sold or transferred, **fair value / market value** must be established through reasonable means (quotations, valuations, public auction, tender).
5. **Segregation of duties** shall be maintained between requesting, approving, executing, and recording disposals.
6. **Sale to related parties or employees** is permitted only at fair market value and with the additional approval levels specified in Section 6.
7. **Data-bearing assets** (e.g., laptops, servers, mobile devices, storage media) must be subject to certified **data sanitization or physical destruction** prior to disposal.
8. Disposal of assets must comply with applicable **environmental, health, safety, and data-protection** laws (e.g., e-waste regulations, PDPA / GDPR).
9. All proceeds from disposals shall be **deposited into the Company's official bank account** — no cash receipts by individuals.
10. The Company shall conduct **periodic physical verification** of fixed assets at least annually to identify assets requiring disposal action.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| **Asset Custodian / Department Head** | Initiates the Disposal Request Form (DRF); provides justification; ensures asset is physically available for verification; ensures data sanitization (for IT assets) is requested. |
| **Department Manager** | Reviews and endorses DRFs originating in the department; confirms operational necessity of disposal. |
| **IT Department** | Performs and certifies data sanitization or destruction for all data-bearing assets; provides technical assessment for IT equipment. |
| **Finance / Fixed Asset Accountant** | Verifies asset existence in FAR, computes NBV, confirms accounting treatment, records disposal entries, removes asset from FAR, reconciles FAR to GL. |
| **Procurement Department** | Sources buyers, obtains quotations, coordinates auction/tender, and executes sale agreements. |
| **Internal Audit** | Independently reviews compliance with this policy on a periodic / sample basis; reports findings to the Audit Committee. |
| **Disposal Committee** | Reviews and recommends disposals above the threshold defined in Section 6. Composition: CFO (Chair), Head of Procurement, Head of relevant business unit, Internal Audit (observer). |
| **CFO / Approving Authority** | Provides final approval based on the Authority Matrix. |
| **External Auditor** | Reviews material disposals as part of statutory audit procedures. |

## 6. Authority Matrix for Disposal Approval

Approval thresholds are based on the **Net Book Value (NBV)** of the asset, or **gross book value** where NBV is zero. Where multiple assets are disposed of in a single transaction, the **aggregate** value applies.

| NBV / Gross Value (whichever is higher) | Initiator | Endorser | Approver | Additional Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ≤ USD 1,000 | Custodian | Department Head | Finance Manager | — |
| > USD 1,000 to ≤ USD 10,000 | Custodian | Department Head | Finance Director / Controller | — |
| > USD 10,000 to ≤ USD 50,000 | Custodian | Department Head | CFO | Disposal Committee review |
| > USD 50,000 to ≤ USD 250,000 | Custodian | CFO | CEO | Disposal Committee recommendation |
| > USD 250,000 | Custodian | CEO | Board of Directors / Audit Committee | Independent valuation required |

**Additional approval is required for:**
- **Sale to employees, directors, or related parties** — one level above the standard matrix and disclosure in related-party transaction register.
- **Donations** — CFO (≤ USD 10,000) or CEO (> USD 10,000) approval required.
- **Write-off due to loss or theft** — CFO approval, supporting police report (where applicable), and Internal Audit review.

## 7. Methods of Disposal

The appropriate method shall be selected by considering value recovery, time, risk, and regulatory requirements:

1. **Sale by Negotiation** — direct sale to a single buyer following at least one written quotation.
2. **Sale by Competitive Quotation** — minimum three written quotations obtained; sale to the highest qualified bidder.
3. **Sealed Tender / Public Auction** — required for disposals with NBV > USD 50,000 unless waived by the Disposal Committee with documented justification.
4. **Trade-In** — exchange against the purchase of a replacement asset; the trade-in value must be benchmarked against an independent quotation.
5. **Scrap / Recycling** — for assets with no economic value; proceeds (if any) from licensed recyclers must be banked.
6. **Donation** — to registered charitable organizations only; requires receipt of acknowledgement letter.
7. **Inter-company / Inter-department Transfer** — at NBV; treated as a transfer (not a disposal) for consolidated reporting but updates location and custodian in the FAR.
8. **Write-Off** — for assets that are lost, stolen, destroyed, or rendered unusable, with no recovery possible.

## 8. Disposal Procedure

### 8.1 Step 1 — Identification and Request

1. The Custodian identifies an asset for disposal and completes the **Disposal Request Form (DRF)** including:
   - Asset description, asset tag/serial number, FAR reference
   - Location, current condition, original cost (if known)
   - Reason for disposal
   - Proposed disposal method
2. The Department Head endorses the DRF and submits it to Finance.

### 8.2 Step 2 — Verification

3. Finance verifies:
   - The asset exists in the FAR.
   - Current NBV, accumulated depreciation, and any impairment.
   - That the asset is not subject to any encumbrance (e.g., pledged as security, under finance lease, government grant restriction).
4. For IT / data-bearing assets, the **IT Department issues a Data Sanitization Certificate** prior to physical disposal.

### 8.3 Step 3 — Valuation (where applicable)

5. For sales, Procurement obtains:
   - At least **one quotation** for disposals ≤ USD 10,000
   - **Three quotations** for disposals > USD 10,000 to ≤ USD 50,000
   - **Tender / auction or independent valuation** for disposals > USD 50,000

### 8.4 Step 4 — Approval

6. The DRF is routed for approval per the Authority Matrix (Section 6).
7. For Disposal Committee reviews, minutes of the meeting and recommendation must be attached.

### 8.5 Step 5 — Execution

8. Upon approval:
   - For sales: Procurement issues invoice, buyer remits payment to the Company's bank account **before** asset release.
   - For scrapping: a licensed disposal vendor is engaged; a **Certificate of Destruction / Recycling** is obtained.
   - For donations: a written acknowledgement is obtained from the recipient.
   - For write-offs: supporting documentation (police report, insurance claim, technical assessment) is attached.
9. Asset tag is removed; physical removal is witnessed by at least one independent party (Finance or Internal Audit representative for disposals > USD 10,000).

### 8.6 Step 6 — Recording

10. Finance posts the disposal accounting entry (see Section 9).
11. Finance updates the FAR — status changed to "Disposed", disposal date, method, proceeds, and gain/loss recorded.
12. The complete disposal file is archived (see Section 11).

### 8.7 Step 7 — Reconciliation

13. The Fixed Asset Accountant reconciles the FAR to the GL at month-end and confirms all approved disposals have been processed.

## 9. Accounting Treatment

Disposals are accounted for in accordance with **IAS 16 / IFRS 16** (or applicable local GAAP):

### 9.1 General Entries

Upon disposal of an asset:

```
Dr  Accumulated Depreciation          (full accumulated depreciation)
Dr  Cash / Bank / Receivable          (sale proceeds, if any)
Dr  Loss on Disposal of Fixed Assets  (if proceeds < NBV)
    Cr  Fixed Asset — Cost             (original cost)
    Cr  Gain on Disposal of Fixed Assets (if proceeds > NBV)
```

### 9.2 Specific Considerations

- **Depreciation** shall be charged up to the **date of disposal** (pro-rated by month or day per Company convention) before the disposal entry is posted.
- **Gains and losses** on disposal are recognized in the income statement in the period of disposal under "Other Income" or "Other Operating Expenses".
- **Revalued assets** — any revaluation surplus attributable to the disposed asset is transferred directly from the revaluation reserve to retained earnings (not through profit or loss).
- **VAT / GST / Sales Tax** — applicable indirect taxes must be computed on the sale proceeds and remitted to the tax authority.
- **Government grants** — disposal of an asset funded by a government grant may trigger refund obligations; Finance must check grant terms before disposal.

## 10. Internal Controls and Segregation of Duties

The following controls are mandatory:

1. **Segregation of Duties** — the person initiating the disposal cannot also approve or record it.
2. **Independent witness** for physical removal of assets with NBV or gross value > USD 10,000.
3. **Sequential numbering** of all DRFs, with a register maintained by Finance.
4. **No cash sales** — all proceeds must flow through the Company's bank account.
5. **Conflict of interest declaration** — any employee or director with a personal interest in the buyer must declare it and recuse themselves from the approval chain.
6. **Quarterly review** of all disposals by the Finance Manager, with sample testing.
7. **Annual physical verification** of fixed assets to identify any unauthorized disposals.
8. **System access controls** — only authorized Finance personnel may post disposal entries in the ERP / accounting system.

## 11. Documentation and Record Retention

The following documents constitute the **Disposal File** and must be retained:

- Disposal Request Form (DRF) with all approvals
- Quotations / tender documents / valuation reports
- Disposal Committee minutes (where applicable)
- Sale invoice and proof of payment receipt
- Certificate of Data Sanitization (for IT assets)
- Certificate of Destruction / Recycling (for scrapping)
- Donation acknowledgement letter (for donations)
- Police report / insurance correspondence (for losses)
- Updated extract from the FAR showing the disposed status
- Journal voucher and supporting calculation of gain / loss

**Retention period:** minimum **seven (7) years** from the date of disposal, or as required by local statute or tax law, whichever is longer.

## 12. Reporting

- **Monthly:** Finance prepares a Fixed Asset Disposal Schedule listing all disposals, proceeds, and gain/loss; submitted to the CFO.
- **Quarterly:** Summary of disposals presented to the Management Committee.
- **Annually:** Disclosure of total disposals, proceeds, and gain/loss in the financial statements, including any material individual disposal.

## 13. Non-Compliance

Any breach of this policy may result in:

- Reversal of unauthorized disposal entries and recovery of assets where possible.
- Personal liability for any financial loss caused.
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.
- Reporting to law enforcement where fraud or theft is suspected.

## 14. Policy Review

This policy shall be reviewed **annually** by the Finance Department and approved by the CFO. Material amendments require ratification by the Audit Committee.

2. Department Head → endorses
3. Finance → verifies FAR, NBV, encumbrances
4. IT (if applicable) → data sanitization certificate
5. Procurement (if sale) → obtain quotations / run tender

7. Approver (per Authority Matrix) → approves
8. Procurement / Custodian → execute disposal, collect proceeds, obtain certificates
9. Finance → post entries, update FAR, archive file
10. Internal Audit → periodic review
